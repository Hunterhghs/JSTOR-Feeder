About the Initiative

H Heuristics is dedicated to producing and disseminating high-quality academic research in the fields of Technology & Innovation.

Through our JSTOR Feeder program, we ensure that rigorous research reaches the widest possible audience. Our publications undergo formal peer review and adhere to academic standards in structure, citations, and methodology.

We believe that knowledge should be accessible. Every report we produce is made freely available through JSTOR's open access initiative, supporting students, researchers, and institutions worldwide.

Core Principles

  • I Rigorous Methodology
  • II Open Access Distribution
  • III Interdisciplinary Approach
  • IV Global Perspective
